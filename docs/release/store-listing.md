# Store listing drafts

These are editable English drafts describing the existing app. Finalize the
publisher identity, support contact, privacy-policy URL, target audience and

## Google Play

**Title:** Ash – Private Collection

**Short description:** Keep a private photo collection of your belongings, values, and maintenance.

**Category:** Productivity

**Full description:**

Keep a personal collection of the things you own.

Add a photo, a name, and a purchase value to start. Organize your belongings
with categories and tags, search your collection, and keep purchase details,
estimated values, notes, and maintenance history together.

- Build a photo collection of your belongings.
- Search, filter, and sort your records.
- Keep maintenance and activity history.
- Track reminders and mark them complete inside the app.
- Review collection values in US dollars.
- Keep your collection on your device, without accounts, ads, or analytics.

Ash does not provide cloud sync or scheduled device notifications. On iOS,
system backups may include app data. Read the privacy policy for retention,
photo access, backups and deletion details.

**Review notes draft:**

No account or sign-in is required. To create an asset, tap Add, choose at least
one photo using the system picker, enter a name and purchase value, then save.
Test photos may be added to the review device's photo library.
Settings contains the offline privacy policy. Reminders are an in-app checklist;
they do not schedule notifications. All calculations run locally.
There are no ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions or developer-operated servers.

## Privacy declarations to review

Based on the current source, Ash does not transmit user data to developer or
third-party services and does not track users. Review the shipped dependencies,
merged Android manifest, and archived iOS privacy manifests before choosing
"No data collected" in Google Play Data safety and App Store App Privacy.

Local photo access and OS backups are explained in the policy. Disclose any
future analytics, networking, crash-reporting SDKs or account features before
shipping them. A store may still request additional declarations. Declare no
ads and no restricted app access if the final build preserves this behavior.
