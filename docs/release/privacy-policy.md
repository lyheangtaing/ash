# Ash privacy policy — publication draft

Before hosting: provide the publisher's legal/developer name and public support
contact, confirm this policy against the shipped build, and remove this draft
notice. Neither a publisher identity nor a contact address has been invented.

## Scope

This policy describes the Android and iOS Ash app, a private collection manager.
Ash has no account registration, advertising, analytics or tracking.

## Records and photos

Ash stores the information you enter on your device: asset names, categories,
tags, values, dates, notes, maintenance/activity records and reminders.

Ash uses the system photo or document picker to access images you select.
It copies selected images into app storage when possible. On Android, when
copying fails, Ash may retain permission to access the selected document.
Ash does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.

## Collection and sharing

Ash does not transmit collection records or selected photos to the developer or
third parties. It has no developer-operated server or cloud synchronization.
Decisions shown on request are calculated locally. Reminders are displayed
inside Ash; they do not schedule device notifications.

Operating systems, app stores and system-selected document/photo providers may
process information under their own policies. They are separate from app-level
collection by Ash.

## Backups and retention

App-managed records and copied photos remain on your device until removed.
Ash disables Android app cloud backups and device transfers. On iOS, system
backups and device transfers may include Ash data according to your system
settings. Original photos remain subject to your photo-library settings.

Deleting a record in Ash removes it from the collection, but associated copies
of photos may remain in app storage. To remove all app-managed records and
copied photos, clear Ash's app storage on Android or delete the app on iOS.
Offloading an iOS app retains its data. Original photos and system backups may
retain copies; manage those separately in your device and backup settings.

## Children

Ash does not collect personal information from children or other users through
developer-operated services. Store audience and age-rating declarations must
accurately reflect the final release and the publisher's intended audience.

## Changes and contact

The publisher should maintain this policy at the public URL registered in both
stores and keep the in-app policy consistent with material changes.

Publisher identity and a public privacy/support contact must be added here
